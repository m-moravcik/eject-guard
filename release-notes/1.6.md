You can stop a Time Machine backup from the menu. While Time Machine is backing up, the popover offers Stop backup, whichever disk it writes to and whether or not you ticked it, say when a backup slows the Mac down. Time Machine carries on at its next backup.

You can start one too. Right-click a Time Machine disk that is plugged in, or a network share, and choose Back up now. The row says Starting backup… until Time Machine picks it up. If a stop or a start does not take, the popover says so, and the warning goes away once the backup is in the state you asked for or the disk is gone.

⌘E only ejects now. Stopping a backup has its own row.

The popover moves the way VibeRes does. A disk gives under the click, and its checkmark bounces once when you guard it. A row that comes or goes, such as a warning, a banner, Stop backup or Undo skip, appears only after the rows around it have made room, so no text runs over another. The backup percentage and the minutes to the meeting roll to their new value, and the countdown now keeps time while the popover is open, where before it showed the minute you opened it on. With Reduce Motion on, rows simply appear and the digits change without rolling.

The welcome tour slides in from the side a step comes from, and no longer jumps in height between steps.
