Eject Guard has its own menu bar icon: an eject triangle over a bar, the app icon in miniature. The bar says what the guard is doing: a thin line when there is nothing to guard, a solid bar while a disk is guarded, and Time Machine's progress, with its clock badge, while a backup runs. When the guard ejects a disk, the triangle crouches, jumps out of the menu bar and lands back on an empty bar. With Reduce Motion on, the icon simply changes.

Signed with Developer ID, notarized and stapled. Requires macOS 14.
